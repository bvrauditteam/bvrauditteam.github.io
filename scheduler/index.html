How it works

WhatsApp doesn't let any website send messages on its own, so this app reminds you and fills in the message — you just tap Send.
  1. When the time comes, you get an alert (keep this app open, or add it to your home screen).
  2. Tap the alert or the green Send now button. WhatsApp opens with the chat and message ready.
  3. Tap send in WhatsApp. Done.

Phones can pause apps in the background. For a reminder that always rings, tap Add to calendar on a message — your phone's calendar will alert you, and the event has the WhatsApp link inside it.

Messages are saved only on this phone/browser.
